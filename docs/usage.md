# Using Companion

What each screen tells you and how to work it. One habit powers everything: **save in game** —
every screen refreshes itself the moment you do.

## The controls

- **Desktop — dwell.** Rest the pointer on anything for a moment and a green bar fills along
  its edge; when it completes, the control fires. No clicking mid-match. Clicking still works.
  A control that is already active never re-fires from dwell — deactivating is a click, or the
  control's own Close.
- **Phone — tap.** On touch screens dwell is off and everything is a plain tap. Tap something
  with extra detail (a pitch card, a set-piece chip) to see its tooltip; tap elsewhere to
  dismiss.
- **Sorting.** Click or dwell any table's column header; again to flip. Position columns sort
  in football order (GK → defence → attack), never alphabetically.
- **Theme.** The Light / Dark / FC26 pill in the header. FC26 matches the game's own menus.

## The screens

### Matchday
Your saved XI against the XI the fit engine would pick, on a pitch drawn from your own
formation's coordinates. The number on each card is that player's rating **in that position** —
at their own position it equals the game's overall exactly; elsewhere it is the overall
adjusted for the move. Amber ring = the engine would pick someone else there. Set-piece chips
(captain, penalties, free kicks, corners) show your current taker against the formula's pick.

### Squad and Youth
The full roster, one screen, the game's own order. Ratings are tiered (blue 80+, green 85+,
violet 90+), the arrow shows the ceiling still reachable, Δ is the change this season. Open a
player for six attribute groups with season change, PlayStyles, wage verdict, contract, and
match record. Youth adds the academy: potential ranges, months in the squad, and the two calls
that matter — sign to senior, or protect in the academy. Scouts live here too, with judgement,
experience, missions, and where to send each next.

### Synergy
Real math over attributes and PlayStyles — who supplies, who receives, how strongly — for your
XI's adjacent pairs, the best partnerships in the whole squad, and pairings that share one job.
**How to raise it** lists the three biggest levers it found: pairs your shape keeps apart, the
highest-synergy signing available, the coldest adjacent pair.

### Transfers
Targets from your save's own world, filtered to players your career can actually sign, grouped
into Superstars / In the making / Wonderkids / Underdogs. Fair fee is fitted on the transfers
this world has actually completed and refuses to price beyond what it has seen. Synergy and Fit
columns are computed against **your** squad.

### Wages
The whole squad, plus any academy prospect the rules want renewed. On a phone the weekly wage
sits beside every name; on desktop the detail pane carries it. "Renewals worth doing" is the filter. Pick a player: two contract shapes (flat, and a
lower base with appearance money), a release-clause verdict, and — when no renewal is proposed —
the plain reason why.

### Loans
Who is out, where, and the only performance signal the save actually records: rating and
ceiling movement since July. Candidates the rules say need football elsewhere come with clubs
where their level makes them a starter, and the deal terms to hold out for.

### Stats
Season records with points pace, position strength, age profile, scorers, ratings, minutes,
season growth, **Ceiling watch** (dynamic potential, observed from your own snapshots — falls
first), the Board's expectation trackers with a This season / History toggle, your record book
(biggest win, worst loss), and a To-do list distilled from all of it.

### Story
The shareable career report: a 1080×1350 card built from your save — record, trophies, win
rate, top scorer, the absurd bit. **Save as image** downloads a PNG; **Copy caption** puts a
paste-ready text on the clipboard. The record book and caption preview sit alongside.

## The alert rail

"Needs attention" collects every action the rules fired — renewals expiring, prospects starved
of minutes, ceilings slipping, academy deals running down — each with the numbers that
produced it. Show/Hide keeps it out of the way during play.

## Honesty rules, so you can trust the screen

- `~` marks a derived value (an estimated date, a derived name) — shown, never used to drive
  advice.
- A dash means the save does not hold that fact. Companion does not fill gaps with guesses.
- Fees and fits come from models fitted on your world; when a player is outside what the model
  has seen, it says so instead of printing a number.
